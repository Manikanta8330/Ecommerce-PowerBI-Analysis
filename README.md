# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and order trends. Interactive dashboards help transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Understand customer segmentation and repeat purchases.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Analyze order statuses, cancellations, and payment methods.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — Measures and calculated columns
- **Data Modeling** — Relationships between customers, products, orders, and order items

## 📊 Dashboard Pages
1. **Sales Performance Dashboard** — Revenue, orders, quantity sold, average order value, and sales trends.
2. **Customer Analytics Dashboard** — Customer growth, city-wise analysis, customer segmentation, and top customers.
3. **Product & Category Dashboard** — Product performance, category revenue, top-selling products, and stock status.
4. **Orders & Payment Dashboard** — Order status, payment methods, order trends, and cancellations.
5. **Customer Value & Retention Dashboard** — Repeat customers, customer segments, and revenue by city.
6. **Executive Summary Dashboard** — Key performance indicators and an overview of business performance.
7. **Business Insights & Recommendations** — Findings and suggested actions based on the analysis.

## 🔍 Key Findings
- **500 orders** recorded in the dataset.
- **116.15M total revenue** displayed by the report's revenue measure.
- **4,549 units** sold.
- **100 customers** included in the customer dataset.
- **95 repeat customers** reported by the customer analysis measures.
- **Electronics** is the top revenue-generating category in the report.

*Note: Figures reflect the current report and its measures.*

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Introduce loyalty programs and personalized offers to encourage repeat purchases.
- Monitor stock levels and replenish products that perform well.
- Investigate order cancellations and improve delivery communication.
- Analyze revenue trends and category performance to identify growth opportunities.
- Monitor payment preferences and payment-related cancellations.
